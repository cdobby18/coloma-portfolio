The Gap Between Watching and Building

Carl Joshua Coloma · August 2026 · 1 min read
Share LinkedIn ↗ Email

The AI engineering journey started with a simple thing: watching. At first, I was just absorbing — hours of professional software engineering and AI content online, the deep technical breakdowns from creators like Gazi, Bryl, and Daniel, full of pipelines, agents, and production-grade thinking.

Most of it went over my head at first. But their breakdowns — the "why" behind the "how" — planted a seed. If they could build these systems, I could start building mine.

You can watch a thousand tutorials, but you only learn by building.

That quote is what finally moved me from passive learning to action. I closed the tabs, opened an editor, and started on my first workflow.

The journey from watching to doing became the real story: inspiration got me curious, but building made it mine.
